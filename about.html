About MADHOUSE

MADHOUSE – THE VAULT is JohnnyDaJoker's curated directory of browser games, retro projects and gaming resources. We aim to help you discover interesting games and understand what has actually been checked. Listings open independent third-party websites; MADHOUSE does not operate those games.

How the Vault works

Browse games by genre, search for a title, save favourites in your own browser and try our MADHOUSE Originals. Third-party game listings take you to the original destination; resources such as directories or platforms are clearly identified separately from games.

What our labels mean

A checked destination means its link was examined, not that every level, controller or mobile device was tested. Some projects need original game files or a free account. New discoveries stay unverified while we examine whether they really launch and what their requirements are.

We want the collection to keep growing without sacrificing trust. If you spot a broken link, have a great browser game to suggest or want a correction, visit our contact page.
