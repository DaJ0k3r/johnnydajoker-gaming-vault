## About
MADHOUSE – THE VAULT is JohnnyDaJoker's curated directory of browser games, retro projects and useful gaming resources. We aim to make it easier to find interesting games, understand their requirements and know what has actually been checked. Listings may link to independent third-party websites; MADHOUSE does not operate those games. GAME HARD. SURVIVE HARDER.

## Privacy
MADHOUSE uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate site measurement. The site stores your favourites, recently visited links, local game additions, wishlist and Test Lab results in your browser. These local records are not sent to MADHOUSE by those features. Outbound game links open independent sites with their own privacy practices. No account is required to browse the Vault. For privacy enquiries, contact JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com.

## Contact
For corrections, broken links, game suggestions and general enquiries, email JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal details in reports.

## Takedown and rights concerns
MADHOUSE links to independently hosted games and resources. If you are a rights holder and believe a listing or artwork infringes your rights, please contact us at JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com. Include the listing URL, identification of the material, your relationship to the rights holder and a way to respond. We will review substantiated concerns and may remove or amend listings. This draft is not a claim that every third-party port is licensed, nor a substitute for legal advice.
