Privacy

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate website measurement. Favourites, recently visited games, Test Lab results, personal wishlist and local imports are saved in your browser using local storage. These browser-local features do not send their saved lists to MADHOUSE. When you follow a game link, the third-party website may have its own privacy practices. You can clear saved local data using your browser's site-data controls. For questions contact JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your favourite games, recently opened destinations, personal wishlist, local imports and Test Lab selections are features of your browser. We use local storage for those lists rather than requiring a MADHOUSE account. You can remove that data by clearing storage for this site in your browser settings; doing so also resets your saved choices.

Analytics and other websites

Cloudflare Web Analytics helps us understand overall traffic and performance. The Vault contains external game links, which open services run by their own operators. Their accounts, advertising, cookies and privacy policies are outside our control; review them before sharing personal details.

Questions or requests

For questions about this page or a listing, contact JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information by email.
