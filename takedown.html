Rights and takedown requests

MADHOUSE is a directory linking to independently operated websites. If you believe a listing or image infringes your rights, email JohnnyDaJokergaming@gmail.com with the relevant Vault URL, a description of the material, your relationship to the rights holder and a way to contact you. We will review substantiated requests and may amend or remove a listing. A listing does not mean MADHOUSE has verified third-party licensing.

What to include in a request

Identify the specific MADHOUSE page or listing, the material you believe is problematic, and enough information to explain your rights or authority to act for the rights holder. If possible, provide a link to the original work or an authoritative source, together with a reliable reply address. Please do not email passwords or confidential account information.

What happens next

We can examine the reported listing, correct its description or link, or remove it where appropriate. MADHOUSE links to many independent game operators and cannot change material hosted on their websites. For material hosted elsewhere, you may also need to contact that provider directly.

For general game suggestions or a broken link that does not involve rights, see our contact page.
